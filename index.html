    How the trial works

    The other avatar applies equipment to your selected target. One item fits each slot. Switching targets keeps both loadouts. Actions share a six-point lab budget; failed attempts also cost AP. Refill AP starts a fresh budget without removing equipment. Release is a free lab override, separate from the probabilistic escape action.

    Application chance = 100 − application difficulty + target condition bonus − 40% of the applying avatar's dexterity penalty (rounded, limited to 5–95%). Distracted adds 15; staggered adds 30. Escape chance = 100 − escape difficulty − 25% of the restrained avatar's dexterity penalty (rounded, limited to 5–90%). An escape attempt costs 2 AP. A roll from 1–100 succeeds at or below the displayed chance.

    Securing methods add to base difficulty, escape difficulty, cost, and debuffs. These are fictional game ratings. Full combat, energy consumption, and elimination are not implemented yet. Use Save roster above to keep both loadouts; trial AP, conditions and log reset on reload.
