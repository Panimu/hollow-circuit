    Trial rules and saving

    The other character applies or strengthens restraints on the chosen target. Both characters share a six-point trial budget. Accepted attempts spend AP even on failure. Refill starts a fresh budget without removing equipment. Free lab releases are separate from escape checks.

    Every slot has three dedicated restraints: rope, leather and steel. Assemblies occupy multiple slots; overlap is rejected without spending AP. Each occupied slot blocks its listed special action. All items also apply their listed attribute penalties, capped at 85%. Escape costs 2 AP and always retains at least a 5% chance. Stronger securing methods can be applied after the initial restraint without removing it.

    This view saves its two loadouts separately from the character studio and pose lab. AP, target condition and history reset on reload. The slot cards and active list show the restraint state; open the character studio for artwork fitting.
